# Service Excellence

Client service grounded in the Charte d'Excellence — the client judges the service, not the code.

## The core reflex

Before sending anything client-facing, check three things: what the recipient will **see**, what they will **conclude**, and what they can **do next**. If any of the three is unclear, the artifact is not ready.

## The five moments of truth

Five moments decide the whole relationship. Prepare them like deliveries:

| Moment | What must be true | Related procedure |
|---|---|---|
| First request | Answer within 24h, even without a solution | Proc. 12 |
| Framing | Pitch written and signed, appetite fixed | Proc. 18 |
| First deliverable | Smallest visible success, delivered early | Proc. 15, 2 |
| Incident | Client informed before they discover; systematic debrief | Proc. 7 |
| Renewal / handover | Explicit final state and handover, nothing implicit | Proc. 4, 6 |

Never let a moment of truth pass unmanaged.

## Client-facing artifacts are deliverables

Meeting notes, status reports, incident updates: factual, dated, explicit next steps with named owners. Sent within the hour for calls and meetings.

## Incident posture

Stabilize, communicate before the client discovers, resolve, learn. Never speculate on causes in client-facing messages before the facts are established. No blame during the incident — the debrief comes after.
